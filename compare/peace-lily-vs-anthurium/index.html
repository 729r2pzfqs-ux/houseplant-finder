Peace Lily vs Anthurium

The peace lily is easier and flowers in far lower light; the anthurium's waxy red spathes last weeks but need a bright spot. Both are toxic to pets.

Peace Lily is rated easy to care for, Anthurium medium. If this is an early houseplant or the spot is hard to get right, start with the Peace Lily. Peace Lily needs low to medium light (2/5) - fine a few metres back from a window. Anthurium wants bright indirect light (4/5), beside a bright window, no midday sun on the leaves.

What they share: both are toxic to cats and dogs, both take moderate watering, both are happy in the same humidity and both stay medium.

Key differences between Peace Lily and Anthurium

Peace Lily is the easier plant

Peace Lily is rated easy to care for, Anthurium medium. If this is an early houseplant or the spot is hard to get right, start with the Peace Lily.

Peace Lily copes with much less light

Peace Lily needs low to medium light (2/5) - fine a few metres back from a window. Anthurium wants bright indirect light (4/5), beside a bright window, no midday sun on the leaves.

How to tell Peace Lily and Anthurium apart

Both throw a spathe around a spadix, but the anthurium's is a waxy red or pink heart that lasts for weeks, while the peace lily's is a white hood that fades to green. The peace lily flowers in much lower light; the anthurium needs a bright spot to rebloom at all.
