Snake Plant vs ZZ Plant

The ZZ plant is the one that truly grows in a dark corner; the snake plant needs some light or it goes leggy. Both want water barely twice a month.

ZZ Plant needs low light (1/5) - survives in a dim corner away from any window. Snake Plant wants low to medium light (2/5), fine a few metres back from a window. Snake Plant originates in West Africa; ZZ Plant in East Africa. Matching the original habitat is the quickest way to guess what each one wants indoors.

What they share: both are toxic to cats and dogs, both are rated easy, both take very low watering, both are happy in the same humidity and both stay medium.

Key differences between Snake Plant and ZZ Plant

ZZ Plant copes with less light

ZZ Plant needs low light (1/5) - survives in a dim corner away from any window. Snake Plant wants low to medium light (2/5), fine a few metres back from a window.

They come from different habitats

Snake Plant originates in West Africa; ZZ Plant in East Africa. Matching the original habitat is the quickest way to guess what each one wants indoors.

How to tell Snake Plant and ZZ Plant apart

Both are stiff, upright and nearly unkillable, so the real split is light. The ZZ plant is the one that genuinely grows in a dark corner; the snake plant tolerates low light but gets leggy and pale there. Snake plant leaves are flat, banded blades from a rosette; ZZ leaves are glossy oval leaflets lined along a thick, arching stalk.
