Monstera Deliciosa vs Rhaphidophora Tetrasperma

Rhaphidophora tetrasperma splits its leaves immediately and stays apartment-sized. Monstera deliciosa needs years, a pole and far more floor space.

Monstera Deliciosa reaches 90-180+ cm (3-6+ ft) and belongs on the floor. Rhaphidophora Tetrasperma tops out around 30-90 cm (1-3 ft), so it suits a side table or plant stand. Monstera Deliciosa originates in Central America; Rhaphidophora Tetrasperma in Thailand, Malaysia. Matching the original habitat is the quickest way to guess what each one wants indoors.

What they share: both are toxic to cats and dogs, both are rated easy, both want bright indirect light, both take moderate watering and both are happy in the same humidity.

Key differences between Monstera Deliciosa and Rhaphidophora Tetrasperma

Monstera Deliciosa is the bigger plant

Monstera Deliciosa reaches 90-180+ cm (3-6+ ft) and belongs on the floor. Rhaphidophora Tetrasperma tops out around 30-90 cm (1-3 ft), so it suits a side table or plant stand.

They come from different habitats

Monstera Deliciosa originates in Central America; Rhaphidophora Tetrasperma in Thailand, Malaysia. Matching the original habitat is the quickest way to guess what each one wants indoors.

How to tell Monstera Deliciosa and Rhaphidophora Tetrasperma apart

The 'mini monstera' is not a monstera at all. Rhaphidophora tetrasperma splits its leaves from day one on a fast, thin vine and stays apartment-sized. Monstera deliciosa needs years and a sturdy pole before it fenestrates, then it keeps getting bigger. Buy the rhaphidophora if you want the look without the floor space.
