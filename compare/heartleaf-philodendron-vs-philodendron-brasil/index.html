Heartleaf Philodendron vs Philodendron Brasil

Brasil is a variegated heartleaf - identical care, plus a lime stripe that fades to plain green unless you give it brighter indirect light.

Heartleaf Philodendron originates in Central America; Philodendron Brasil in South America. Matching the original habitat is the quickest way to guess what each one wants indoors.

What they share: both are toxic to cats and dogs, both are rated easy, both want medium indirect light, both take low watering, both are happy in the same humidity and both stay medium.
