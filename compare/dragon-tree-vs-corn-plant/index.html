Dragon Tree vs Corn Plant

The corn plant handles lower light and grows bigger, with wide striped leaves; the dragon tree stays narrow with thin red-edged blades. Both are easy.

Corn Plant needs low to medium light (2/5) - fine a few metres back from a window. Dragon Tree wants medium indirect light (3/5), near a bright window, out of direct sun. Dragon Tree originates in Madagascar; Corn Plant in Tropical Africa. Matching the original habitat is the quickest way to guess what each one wants indoors.

What they share: both are toxic to cats and dogs, both are rated easy, both take low watering, both are happy in the same humidity and both stay large.

Key differences between Dragon Tree and Corn Plant

Corn Plant copes with less light

Corn Plant needs low to medium light (2/5) - fine a few metres back from a window. Dragon Tree wants medium indirect light (3/5), near a bright window, out of direct sun.

They come from different habitats

Dragon Tree originates in Madagascar; Corn Plant in Tropical Africa. Matching the original habitat is the quickest way to guess what each one wants indoors.

How to tell Dragon Tree and Corn Plant apart

The dragon tree has thin, arching, red-edged blades; the corn plant has wide, strappy leaves with a pale central stripe on thicker canes. The corn plant handles low light better and gets bigger; the dragon tree stays narrower.
