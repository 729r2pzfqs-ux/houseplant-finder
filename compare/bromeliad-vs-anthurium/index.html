Bromeliad vs Anthurium

The bromeliad is pet-safe but flowers once and dies, leaving pups. The anthurium reblooms for years from the same plant but is toxic to pets.

Bromeliad is non-toxic to cats and dogs. Anthurium is toxic to cats and dogs if chewed, so it needs a shelf pets cannot reach. Bromeliad is rated easy to care for, Anthurium medium. If this is an early houseplant or the spot is hard to get right, start with the Bromeliad.

What they share: both are happy in the same humidity and both stay medium.

Key differences between Bromeliad and Anthurium

Bromeliad is pet-safe, Anthurium is not

Bromeliad is non-toxic to cats and dogs. Anthurium is toxic to cats and dogs if chewed, so it needs a shelf pets cannot reach.

Bromeliad is the easier plant

Bromeliad is rated easy to care for, Anthurium medium. If this is an early houseplant or the spot is hard to get right, start with the Bromeliad.

Bromeliad copes with less light

Bromeliad needs medium indirect light (3/5) - near a bright window, out of direct sun. Anthurium wants bright indirect light (4/5), beside a bright window, no midday sun on the leaves.

Anthurium drinks more

Bromeliad wants low watering (2/5) - water when the top 3-5 cm of soil has dried out. Anthurium needs moderate watering (3/5): water when the top 2-3 cm is dry, roughly weekly.

Anthurium fills out faster

Anthurium is a moderate grower and gives you cuttings or a full pot sooner. Bromeliad is slow, which means less pruning and repotting but a longer wait.

Anthurium is the air-purifying one

Anthurium appears on the common indoor air-purifying lists; Bromeliad does not. In a normal room the effect of either is modest, so treat it as a tiebreaker rather than a reason to buy.

How to tell Bromeliad and Anthurium apart

The guzmania's colour is a bract that lasts for months, then the mother plant dies and leaves pups behind -- it is a one-show plant that replaces itself. The anthurium keeps producing new flowers from the same plant for years. Both are easy; only the guzmania is pet-safe.
