Golden Pothos vs Marble Queen Pothos

Marble Queen carries much more white, so it grows slower and needs brighter indirect light. Golden pothos is the faster, darker-corner-tolerant one.

Golden Pothos needs low to medium light (2/5) - fine a few metres back from a window. Marble Queen Pothos wants medium indirect light (3/5), near a bright window, out of direct sun. Golden Pothos is a fast grower and gives you cuttings or a full pot sooner. Marble Queen Pothos is moderate, which means less pruning and repotting but a longer wait.

What they share: both are toxic to cats and dogs, both are rated easy, both take low watering, both are happy in the same humidity and both stay medium.

How to tell Golden Pothos and Marble Queen Pothos apart

Same plant, different amount of white. Marble Queen carries far more variegation, which means less chlorophyll, which means it grows noticeably slower and wants brighter indirect light to hold its pattern. Golden pothos is the faster, more forgiving of the two.
