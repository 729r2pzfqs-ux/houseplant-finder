Watermelon Peperomia vs Aluminum Plant

The peperomia's silver runs in clean stripes and it stores water in its leaves; the aluminum plant's is a quilted splash and it wants steadier moisture.

Watermelon Peperomia wants low watering (2/5) - water when the top 3-5 cm of soil has dried out. Aluminum Plant needs moderate watering (3/5): water when the top 2-3 cm is dry, roughly weekly. Aluminum Plant is a fast grower and gives you cuttings or a full pot sooner. Watermelon Peperomia is slow, which means less pruning and repotting but a longer wait.

What they share: both are pet-safe, both are rated easy, both want medium indirect light, both are happy in the same humidity and both stay small.

How to tell Watermelon Peperomia and Aluminum Plant apart

Both are small plants with silver markings, but the patterns differ: the watermelon peperomia's silver runs in clean stripes from the stalk, the aluminum plant's is a quilted splash between the veins. The aluminum plant grows faster, wants steadier moisture and needs pinching to stay bushy.
