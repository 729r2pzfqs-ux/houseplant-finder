Key differences between Bamboo Palm and Lady Palm

They come from different habitats

Bamboo Palm originates in Mexico; Lady Palm in China. Matching the original habitat is the quickest way to guess what each one wants indoors.

How to tell Bamboo Palm and Lady Palm apart

The lady palm is the more refined and more expensive of the two, with stiff, fan-shaped leaflets and a very slow, dense habit. The bamboo palm is softer, feathery and cheaper, and fills a corner faster. Both tolerate low light and both are pet-safe.

Which is easier to care for, Bamboo Palm or Lady Palm? +

Neither - both are rated easy. The practical difference is they come from different habitats.

Is Bamboo Palm or Lady Palm safe for cats and dogs? +

Both are safe. Bamboo Palm and Lady Palm are each non-toxic to cats and dogs, so either works in a home with animals.

Which needs less light, Bamboo Palm or Lady Palm? +

They are the same. Both score 2/5 for light, meaning fine a few metres back from a window.

Which needs more water, Bamboo Palm or Lady Palm? +

Neither - both score 3/5. For each one, water when the top 2-3 cm is dry, roughly weekly.
