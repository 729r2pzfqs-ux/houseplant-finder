Baby Rubber Plant vs Rubber Plant

The baby rubber plant is a pet-safe shelf peperomia, not a small Ficus elastica. The real rubber plant becomes a floor tree and is toxic to pets.

Baby Rubber Plant is non-toxic to cats and dogs. Rubber Plant is toxic to cats and dogs if chewed, so it needs a shelf pets cannot reach. Baby Rubber Plant needs medium indirect light (3/5) - near a bright window, out of direct sun. Rubber Plant wants bright indirect light (4/5), beside a bright window, no midday sun on the leaves.

What they share: both are rated easy and both take low watering.

Key differences between Baby Rubber Plant and Rubber Plant

Baby Rubber Plant is pet-safe, Rubber Plant is not

Baby Rubber Plant is non-toxic to cats and dogs. Rubber Plant is toxic to cats and dogs if chewed, so it needs a shelf pets cannot reach.

Baby Rubber Plant copes with less light

Baby Rubber Plant needs medium indirect light (3/5) - near a bright window, out of direct sun. Rubber Plant wants bright indirect light (4/5), beside a bright window, no midday sun on the leaves.

Rubber Plant needs damper air

Rubber Plant rates 3/5 for humidity - normal household humidity works. Baby Rubber Plant rates 2/5, so ordinary room air is fine.

Rubber Plant is the bigger plant

Rubber Plant reaches 90-180+ cm (3-6+ ft) and belongs on the floor. Baby Rubber Plant tops out around 15-30 cm (6-12 in), so it suits a shelf or desk.

Rubber Plant fills out faster

Rubber Plant is a moderate grower and gives you cuttings or a full pot sooner. Baby Rubber Plant is slow, which means less pruning and repotting but a longer wait.

They come from different habitats

Baby Rubber Plant originates in Central America; Rubber Plant in Southeast Asia. Matching the original habitat is the quickest way to guess what each one wants indoors.

How to tell Baby Rubber Plant and Rubber Plant apart

The baby rubber plant is not a small rubber plant -- it is a peperomia with thick, rounded, glossy leaves that stays on a shelf, and it is safe for pets. The real rubber plant (Ficus elastica) becomes a floor tree and is toxic to cats and dogs.

Baby Rubber Plant vs Rubber Plant: common questions

What is the difference between Baby Rubber Plant and Rubber Plant? +

The baby rubber plant is not a small rubber plant -- it is a peperomia with thick, rounded, glossy leaves that stays on a shelf, and it is safe for pets. The real rubber plant (Ficus elastica) becomes a floor tree and is toxic to cats and dogs. On care, baby Rubber Plant is non-toxic to cats and dogs. Rubber Plant is toxic to cats and dogs if chewed, so it needs a shelf pets cannot reach.

Which is easier to care for, Baby Rubber Plant or Rubber Plant? +

Neither - both are rated easy. The practical difference is baby rubber plant is pet-safe, rubber plant is not.

Is Baby Rubber Plant or Rubber Plant safe for cats and dogs? +

Baby Rubber Plant is non-toxic to cats and dogs. Rubber Plant is toxic to cats and dogs if chewed, so keep it out of reach or choose the Baby Rubber Plant.

Which needs less light, Baby Rubber Plant or Rubber Plant? +

Baby Rubber Plant. It scores 3/5 for light and near a bright window, out of direct sun. Rubber Plant scores 4/5 and beside a bright window, no midday sun on the leaves.

Which needs more water, Baby Rubber Plant or Rubber Plant? +

Neither - both score 2/5. For each one, water when the top 3-5 cm of soil has dried out.
