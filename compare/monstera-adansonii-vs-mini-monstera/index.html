Key differences between Monstera Adansonii and Rhaphidophora Tetrasperma

Monstera Adansonii copes with less light

Monstera Adansonii needs medium indirect light (3/5) - near a bright window, out of direct sun. Rhaphidophora Tetrasperma wants bright indirect light (4/5), beside a bright window, no midday sun on the leaves.

Different plant types: trailing vs foliage

Monstera Adansonii is a trailing plant and Rhaphidophora Tetrasperma is a foliage plant, so they fill different jobs in a room even when their care overlaps.

They come from different habitats

Monstera Adansonii originates in Central America; Rhaphidophora Tetrasperma in Thailand, Malaysia. Matching the original habitat is the quickest way to guess what each one wants indoors.

How to tell Monstera Adansonii and Rhaphidophora Tetrasperma apart

The clearest tell is the hole. Adansonii leaves have holes sealed inside the leaf blade; tetrasperma leaves are cut through to the edge, like a tiny deliciosa. Tetrasperma also grows noticeably faster and wants a little more light.
