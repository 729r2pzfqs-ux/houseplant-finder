Hoya vs Peperomia Hope

Peperomia Hope is smaller, faster and tidier; hoya carnosa is slower and woodier but eventually flowers. Both pet-safe, both happiest left to dry out.

Peperomia Hope needs medium indirect light (3/5) - near a bright window, out of direct sun. Hoya wants bright indirect light (4/5), beside a bright window, no midday sun on the leaves. Hoya reaches 30-90 cm (1-3 ft) and belongs on a side table or plant stand. Peperomia Hope tops out around 15-30 cm (6-12 in), so it suits a shelf or desk.

What they share: both are pet-safe, both are rated easy, both take low watering and both are happy in the same humidity.

Key differences between Hoya and Peperomia Hope

Peperomia Hope copes with less light

Peperomia Hope needs medium indirect light (3/5) - near a bright window, out of direct sun. Hoya wants bright indirect light (4/5), beside a bright window, no midday sun on the leaves.

Hoya is the bigger plant

Hoya reaches 30-90 cm (1-3 ft) and belongs on a side table or plant stand. Peperomia Hope tops out around 15-30 cm (6-12 in), so it suits a shelf or desk.

Peperomia Hope fills out faster

Peperomia Hope is a moderate grower and gives you cuttings or a full pot sooner. Hoya is slow, which means less pruning and repotting but a longer wait.

They come from different habitats

Hoya originates in Southeast Asia; Peperomia Hope in Central America. Matching the original habitat is the quickest way to guess what each one wants indoors.

How to tell Hoya and Peperomia Hope apart

Peperomia Hope is the smaller, faster, tidier of the two, with round trailing leaves in threes. Hoya carnosa is slower and woodier but eventually flowers. Both are semi-succulent, pet-safe and prefer to dry out between waterings.
