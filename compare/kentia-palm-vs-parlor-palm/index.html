Kentia Palm vs Parlor Palm

Both take low light and both are pet-safe. The parlor palm is cheap and tabletop-sized; the kentia is a slow, expensive floor palm that lasts decades.

Kentia Palm wants low watering (2/5) - water when the top 3-5 cm of soil has dried out. Parlor Palm needs moderate watering (3/5): water when the top 2-3 cm is dry, roughly weekly. Parlor Palm rates 3/5 for humidity - normal household humidity works. Kentia Palm rates 2/5, so ordinary room air is fine.

What they share: both are pet-safe, both are rated easy and both want low to medium light.

Key differences between Kentia Palm and Parlor Palm

Parlor Palm drinks more

Kentia Palm wants low watering (2/5) - water when the top 3-5 cm of soil has dried out. Parlor Palm needs moderate watering (3/5): water when the top 2-3 cm is dry, roughly weekly.

Parlor Palm needs damper air

Parlor Palm rates 3/5 for humidity - normal household humidity works. Kentia Palm rates 2/5, so ordinary room air is fine.

Kentia Palm is the bigger plant

Kentia Palm reaches 90-180+ cm (3-6+ ft) and belongs on the floor. Parlor Palm tops out around 30-90 cm (1-3 ft), so it suits a side table or plant stand.

They come from different habitats

Kentia Palm originates in Lord Howe Island; Parlor Palm in Mexico. Matching the original habitat is the quickest way to guess what each one wants indoors.

How to tell Kentia Palm and Parlor Palm apart

Same low-light tolerance, very different scale and price. The parlor palm is cheap, tabletop-sized and a little short-lived indoors. The kentia is an expensive, slow-growing floor palm that will still look good in ten years. Both are pet-safe.
