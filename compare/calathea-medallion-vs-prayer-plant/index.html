Calathea Medallion vs Prayer Plant

The prayer plant is the forgiving one - medium not hard, fine in ordinary room humidity and tap water. The calathea crisps without filtered water.

Prayer Plant is rated medium to care for, Calathea Medallion hard. If this is an early houseplant or the spot is hard to get right, start with the Prayer Plant. Calathea Medallion reaches 30-90 cm (1-3 ft) and belongs on a side table or plant stand. Prayer Plant tops out around 15-30 cm (6-12 in), so it suits a shelf or desk.

What they share: both are pet-safe, both want medium indirect light, both take high watering and both are happy in the same humidity.
