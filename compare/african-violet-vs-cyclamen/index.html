African Violet vs Cyclamen

The African violet is pet-safe and blooms indoors year-round for years. The cyclamen is a winter plant that goes dormant, and its tubers are toxic.

African Violet is non-toxic to cats and dogs. Cyclamen is toxic to cats and dogs if chewed, so it needs a shelf pets cannot reach. African Violet rates 4/5 for humidity - group plants or run a humidifier in winter. Cyclamen rates 3/5, so normal household humidity works.

What they share: both are rated medium, both want medium indirect light, both take moderate watering and both stay small.

Key differences between African Violet and Cyclamen

African Violet is pet-safe, Cyclamen is not

African Violet is non-toxic to cats and dogs. Cyclamen is toxic to cats and dogs if chewed, so it needs a shelf pets cannot reach.

African Violet needs damper air

African Violet rates 4/5 for humidity - group plants or run a humidifier in winter. Cyclamen rates 3/5, so normal household humidity works.

They come from different habitats

African Violet originates in East Africa; Cyclamen in Mediterranean. Matching the original habitat is the quickest way to guess what each one wants indoors.

How to tell African Violet and Cyclamen apart

The African violet blooms indoors year-round in modest light and lives for years. The cyclamen is a winter plant that goes fully dormant in summer and is usually treated as seasonal. Cyclamen tubers are toxic to pets; African violets are not.
