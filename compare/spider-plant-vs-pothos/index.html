Spider Plant vs Golden Pothos

The spider plant is pet-safe and hands you free plantlets; pothos tolerates darker corners and trails longer but is toxic to cats and dogs.

Spider Plant is non-toxic to cats and dogs. Golden Pothos is toxic to cats and dogs if chewed, so it needs a shelf pets cannot reach. Golden Pothos needs low to medium light (2/5) - fine a few metres back from a window. Spider Plant wants medium indirect light (3/5), near a bright window, out of direct sun.

What they share: both are rated easy, both are happy in the same humidity and both stay medium.

Key differences between Spider Plant and Golden Pothos

Spider Plant is pet-safe, Golden Pothos is not

Spider Plant is non-toxic to cats and dogs. Golden Pothos is toxic to cats and dogs if chewed, so it needs a shelf pets cannot reach.

Golden Pothos copes with less light

Golden Pothos needs low to medium light (2/5) - fine a few metres back from a window. Spider Plant wants medium indirect light (3/5), near a bright window, out of direct sun.

Spider Plant drinks more

Golden Pothos wants low watering (2/5) - water when the top 3-5 cm of soil has dried out. Spider Plant needs moderate watering (3/5): water when the top 2-3 cm is dry, roughly weekly.

They come from different habitats

Spider Plant originates in South Africa; Golden Pothos in Southeast Asia. Matching the original habitat is the quickest way to guess what each one wants indoors.

How to tell Spider Plant and Golden Pothos apart

The spider plant is the pet-safe option and the one that makes babies you can root for free. Pothos tolerates darker corners and trails longer, but it is toxic to cats and dogs. For a bright kitchen with animals, take the spider plant.
