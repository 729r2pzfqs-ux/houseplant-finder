Key differences between Hoya and Hoya Kerrii

Different plant types: trailing vs succulent

Hoya is a trailing plant and Hoya Kerrii is a succulent plant, so they fill different jobs in a room even when their care overlaps.

How to tell Hoya and Hoya Kerrii apart

Carnosa is the vining hoya that eventually flowers in porcelain-like clusters. Kerrii is the heart-shaped one -- and the single leaf sold at Valentine's usually has no node, so it will sit there for years and never vine. Buy a kerrii with a stem if you want a plant rather than an ornament.

Which is easier to care for, Hoya or Hoya Kerrii? +

Neither - both are rated easy. The practical difference is different plant types: trailing vs succulent.

Is Hoya or Hoya Kerrii safe for cats and dogs? +

Both are safe. Hoya and Hoya Kerrii are each non-toxic to cats and dogs, so either works in a home with animals.

Which needs less light, Hoya or Hoya Kerrii? +

They are the same. Both score 4/5 for light, meaning beside a bright window, no midday sun on the leaves.

Which needs more water, Hoya or Hoya Kerrii? +

Neither - both score 2/5. For each one, water when the top 3-5 cm of soil has dried out.
